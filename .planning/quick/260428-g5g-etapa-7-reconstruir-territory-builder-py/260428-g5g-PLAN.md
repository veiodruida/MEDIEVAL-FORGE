<objective>
Etapa 7 (master plan step H.7): Reconstruct `territory_builder.py` so it can aggregate
OSM-derived baronies (from `baronies_builder.build_baronies_from_osm`) into condados
according to `MapResearchResult.barony_assignments` (Etapa 3 schema), producing the
generator-ready `CONDADOS = [(id, name, lon, lat, duchy_id, baronies)]` tuples — where
each condado's centroid is the MEAN of its member-barony centroids.

This restores the CK3-style architecture: baronies are organic OSM polygons; condados
are political aggregations whose coordinates derive from member geography (not
LLM-invented). Without this step, the new pipeline would still feed the old
LLM-invented coords into `map_generator.py`, perpetuating the "erro 0" crash.

Purpose: Close the geometry gap between Etapa 2 (baronies_builder) + Etapa 6
(MapResearchResult / barony_assignments) and the existing `services/generator.py`
contract. After this step, a future small task can wire `research_runner.py` to call
`build_map_research_prompt` + the new aggregator.

Output:
- New function `assemble_territory_data_from_baronies(map_research_payload, baronies_geojson)`
  in `services/territory_builder.py`.
- 4 new tests in `backend/tests/services/test_territory_builder_baronies_aggregation.py`.
- Existing `assemble_territory_data` and `build_territory_data_from_cache` remain
  untouched for backwards compatibility.
</objective>
