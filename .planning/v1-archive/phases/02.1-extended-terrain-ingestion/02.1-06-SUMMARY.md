## What Was Built

**`useTerrainStepStream(projectId, step)`** — AbortController-driven SSE consumer hook:
- 4 status states: `pendente | rodando | pronto | erro`
- `start(params?)` clears state and opens SSE stream to `/api/projects/{id}/terrain/{step}`
- `stop()` calls `/terrain/stop?step=X` (server-side scoped cancel, T-01-04), then aborts client fetch
- Ridge sensitivity threads as `?sensitivity=low|med|high` query param
- Re-run semantics: clears local state on every new `start()` (D-26)
- AbortError filtered — user-initiated stop sets status back to `pendente`, not `erro`

**`TerrainDataSection`** — Collapsible section component:
- Header: "Terrain Data" heading + `N/4 datasets prontos` badge (collapsed by default, D-24)
- 4 StepCards in order: Overpass terrain, HydroSHEDS basins, DEM (Copernicus), Ridges (derive)
- Per-card: status Badge (pendente/rodando/pronto/erro) + Executar/Parar button
- Ridges card: Radix SegmentedControl with low/med/high options (D-17)
- Shared SSE log panel below all cards shows active step lines (D-27)
- Outermost `<Card>` from `@radix-ui/themes` (Pitfall 8 transparency mitigation)

**ProjectDetail wiring:** `<TerrainDataSection projectId={id} />` inserted immediately above `<Stepper>` (D-24).
